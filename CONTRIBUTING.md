Contributors must show a full and clear understanding of their contribution. The mod author reserves the right to reject any contribution at any time if they determine that this isn't the case.

Contributions that are AI generated and submitted without review or understanding will be rejected. This includes:
- Titles, descriptions, contents and code entirely generated by AI
- Replying to questions or review comments with AI output
- Any content in the contribution that you can't explain or defend

Using AI translation tools to communicate in English is allowed.

Repeated violations of these guidelines will result in a ban from the author's repositories
and the Discord server for their modding projects.
